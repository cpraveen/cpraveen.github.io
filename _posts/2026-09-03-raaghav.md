**Title**: Numerical development of topological and shock singularities in fluids  
**Speaker**: Raaghav Ramani, LANL  
**Date**: 10 September 2026
**Time**: 10 AM IST  
**Venue**: Zoom

**Abstract**: In the first part of this talk, I will describe a new method for resolving topological singularities, such as coalescence and pinching, in evolving fluid interfaces. The method uses a hybrid sharp--diffuse interface representation, with localized Cahn--Hilliard dynamics during topological events. In the second part, based on joint work with Steve Shkoller, I will describe a numerical method for tracking the formation and subsequent development of one-dimensional shocks, together with the associated weak characteristic discontinuities and cusp structures. This method uses a moving mesh adapted to the evolving characteristic wave families. In both settings, I will demonstrate orders-of-magnitude gains in accuracy and efficiency over conventional numerical schemes.
